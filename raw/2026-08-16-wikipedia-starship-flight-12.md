# Starship flight test 12

Reduced from the Wikipedia article on 16 August 2026.

## Launch

- Date and time: 22 May 2026, 22:30:22 UTC (17:30:22 CDT)
- Booster: Super Heavy B19 (Block 3)
- Ship: Starship S39 (Block 3)

## Payload

20 Starlink V3 mass simulators plus 2 modified Starlink V2, total mass approximately 37,500 kg. Deployed with an improved "Pez dispenser" mechanism.

> Note on a source disagreement: this article describes the two working satellites as **modified Starlink V2**. The [List of Starship launches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Starship_launches) article describes them as **V3**. Recorded rather than resolved; it does not affect the total mass.

## Trajectory

Suborbital, deliberately placed slightly short of orbital velocity, on a southerly track that would direct any debris over the Caribbean.

## Outcomes

- **Booster:** failed. After stage separation it flipped abnormally fast and struck the Gulf of Mexico at 1,450 km/h rather than landing near the launch site.
- **Ship:** met its primary objective and splashed down in the Indian Ocean as planned.

## Firsts

First flight of Block 3 vehicles, and first launch from Starbase's second launch pad.
