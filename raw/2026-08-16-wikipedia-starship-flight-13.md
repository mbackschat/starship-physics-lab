# Starship flight test 13

Reduced from the Wikipedia article on 16 August 2026.

## Launch

- Date and time: 24 July 2026, 22:51 UTC
- Booster: Super Heavy B20 (Block 3)
- Ship: Starship S40 (Block 3)

## Payload

20 Starlink V3 satellites, approximately 34,100 kg total. The first Starship flight to deploy operational satellites rather than simulators.

## Outcomes

- **Booster:** failed its landing. Only 10 of 13 Raptor engines relit for the landing burn, and it was destroyed on impact in the Gulf of Mexico.
- **Ship:** succeeded. Splashed down in the Indian Ocean and remained intact and operational after tipping over, a first for the vehicle, which allowed the heat shield and engines to be inspected by drone afterwards.

## Firsts

First operational satellite deployment. First upper stage to survive splashdown intact.
