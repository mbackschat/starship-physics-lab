# Starship Flight 14, as planned

Reduced from reporting retrieved on 16 August 2026, cross-checked against [Space.com's account of SpaceX's August 2026 earnings call](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/spacex-wants-to-launch-next-starship-this-month-and-catch-it-too-elon-musk-says-in-1st-earnings-call-since-historic-ipo).

**As of 16 August 2026 this flight had not launched.**

## Schedule

Tentatively targeting late August 2026. Unconfirmed, pending SpaceX readiness and FAA regulatory approval.

## Vehicles

Booster 21 (B21) and Ship 41 (S41). The third flight of Block 3 vehicles, after Flight 12 (B19/S39) and Flight 13 (B20/S40).

## Mission

Three firsts on one flight:

1. **First orbital attempt.** Every previous flight was deliberately placed short of orbital velocity. This one targets an operational orbit.
2. **First ship catch.** The tower's arms would catch the 50 m upper stage, having previously caught only boosters.
3. **Booster returning to the launch site**, rather than to the Gulf.

Payload is the first batch of operational Starlink V3 satellites flown to a real orbit.

## Why it matters here

This produces the first direct measurement of what Starship delivers to orbit, which is the number the payload argument turns on. The prediction made before the flight is pre-registered in [studies/flight-14-prediction/](../studies/flight-14-prediction/finding.md).
